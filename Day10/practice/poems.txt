        “Quiet Fire”


You carry storms behind your eyes,
Yet speak like morning light.
The world may rush, demand, disguise—
You still choose what is right.

Not every strength is loud or seen,
Not every wound will show.
But roots grow deep beneath the green,
And silent rivers flow.

So when the night feels far too long,
And doubt begins to climb,
Remember: even broken songs
Can turn to gold with time.
